## Prompt rendering, personas and roles

- Compatibility full/debug and CLI prompts are wrapped once at model delivery with the current-time line, then enabled confirmed slang, then the wake prompt. The current-time line uses `time.zone` for both the local time and its label. `host` follows the machine and `TZ` where applicable. An IANA name follows its calendar rules at the time shown. Their section order is role (closed-agent only), persona (`【人设】`, companion modes only), identity, token, bootstrap initial prompt, fixed guidance, state sections and reason tail. In live reserved2, both turn engines use the briefing and wake-delta contract below; reminders retain their own body. Automatic recall (`memory::recall`: up to `memory.inject.relatedTopK` memories (default 3, capped at five per wake) qualified by sparse or local semantic relevance. Sparse recall matches the message being answered after removing common Chinese stopwords and requires two distinct shared terms covering at least four distinct characters, one identifier covering at least four letters or digits, or one word covering at least six. Semantic recall embeds that message with up to four current speaker names and the most recently mentioned active topic on the host. Only in-scope matches past either relevance bar enter reciprocal rank fusion. Messages under four letters, digits or Han characters recall nothing. Unavailable semantic recall uses the sparse ranking, with pin, importance, recency and past use adding at most 0.05. Recall is never padded with pinned or recent rows, and never includes one the session generation already carries (the installed briefing's `recalled` ids, kept across a same-generation full refresh and reset by a new generation, session or epoch; DSH holds recall once it accepts a wake, and the harness holds it once the canonical wake completes), plus the index hint; none when `memory.enabled` is false; the compatibility render holds nothing back) is carried by the compatibility full render and the live DSH and harness wake deltas. The console preview suppresses recall held in the DSH session map. It can show memories already held in harness history. Wake reminders and the text-only CLI prompt (`build_text_only`) carry none. Rendering never counts search hits. Each recalled memory counts one hit per delivery of a prompt carrying it: when DSH accepts a submission (the wake delta, or the compatibility render where no delta is rendered), when the harness accepts the first usable model response for the wake, or when a CLI fallback backend answers for the compatibility render. A refused, failed or unaccepted attempt counts nothing, and a retried attempt counts only when it is accepted.

Final slot forces full observation timeout (ignoring shorter `timeoutMs`/purpose/minNewMessages); final and later results carry `waitLimitReached=true`, `nextAction="end_turn"`, `"end this turn now: produce your final answer and make no further tool calls"`, returning immediately when exhausted without observation. Exhausted reserved2 turns complete as `observationSleep` (even if final wait delivered a `forYou` message), keeping wake config, rearming finite deadline, skipping closure reminders/no-action reset; closeout tools stay usable with normal authority.
- **Engagement window:** configured observation duration post last successful send (`engagedAt`, cleared at wake claim; started by delivered text/voice/sticker/poke; applies in private too, where mandatory closeout observation is exempt; incoming chatter/skipped messages/other pokes never extend). Pre-sleep never outlasts window; agent-token messages wait runs to window end (clipped by `wait.maxMs` unless forced observation exceeds it, and by turn budget), returning immediately without batching on `forYou` or urgent @/owner arrival, or collecting chatter to window end (re-checked against current send/deadline on return). Window-end chatter sets `engagementEnded=true`, `preSleepWaitSatisfied=true`, `turnBudgetEnded` and ends turn (`observationSleep`, wake config kept, no closure/no-action/cancel). `<1000 ms` remaining hands over waiting `forYou`/urgent message (refunding slot normally) or ends immediately with waiting chatter (no refund). Urgent @/owner or `forYou` anywhere in result write never ends turn (1 response step, no refund, next wait refused). Wait hitting `waitLimitReached` (and refused wait after it; storage failures logged, limit result still returned) also sets `turnBudgetEnded`.
- **`forYou` & slot refund (`question::ingress_for_you`):** non-poke private (`私聊你`), poke targeting her (`拍了拍你`), or group quote-to-her / @her / nickname / group-card / exchange rule (member she replied/@'d or who quoted her within `questionExchangeWindowMs`, without @ing another member). Judged against pre-message state (unreadable state logs and drops exchange evidence; older records fall back to private/quote/@ facts). Wait results carrying `forYou` refund slot up to `socialV2.send.maxPerTurn` (default 20) times per ledger turn; timeout/chatter keep spending 1. Briefings show `【互动窗口】` only while active.

## Briefing and wake delta

- **Delivery (`briefing_delivery.rs`, `session_context.rs`, `session_map.rs`, `pipeline/dsh_adapter.rs`):** full briefing once per session generation (`begin` -> gen 0 on rotation/reset; `next_generation` on compaction/persona switch), then deltas with section replacements/removals. Selected at actual sid/epoch resolution (incl. mid-dispatch replacement) and persisted in `sessions.json` `briefings` (format 1) under mapping+social gates.
  - DSH ack requires observed user/message in same sid/epoch/generation (`requestId` defaults to tracked envelope ID; echoed in `data.source.rpcId`, fixture `tests/fixtures/dsh-0.2.1-rc.2-user-message.json`); pending RPC is not proof. Completed compaction / summary-checkpoint replacement invalidates proof once per compaction ID; prune-only/image-offload do not; unobserved forward history gap forces 1 full briefing; no periodic refresh.
  - Harness replays generation facts (supplying full briefing if clipped) and gets receipt on completed canonical user fact.
- **Sections & memory layers (`briefing.rs`):** Identity (`identity`: role, `briefing::persona_core`, bot identity, role hint) and Relationship (`operator`, `impressions`) are never cut (overruns deduct from Material, then Conversation). Conversation (`memory` topics/thoughts, `participation`, `slang`) cuts backwards from end (`slang` -> `participation` -> oldest thoughts/topics). Material (delta auto-recall + `【记忆索引】` <=60 est. tokens) keeps top-ranked fits; cut memories are unrendered/unheld/uncounted.
  - Defaults: `memory.layers.{identityTokens:1500, relationshipTokens:1500, conversationTokens:800, materialTokens:1000, materialItems:5}` (estimated tokens: 1/non-ASCII, 1/4 ASCII; `materialItems` tracks valid `relatedTopK`). Rule sections (`behaviour`/`stickers`/`sending`/`reply`/`sleep`/`retrieval`/`wakeWords`) sit outside layers. Stickers list `id：note` for up to `socialV2.sticker.promptMaxStickers` (default 8) top stickers without use counts (inventory shown when sticker/`[表情N]` softens reply). Ledger `attempt` records per-layer `{injected, cut}`. Empty Identity (any conv) or empty Relationship (owner private) alerts once per `(conv, sid, epoch, gen, layer)` via `admin_alert::notify_memory`.
- **Member impressions:** ordered newest-speaking in unread/recent (by QQ id or display name), then by `lastSeenAt`, capped at `socialV2.context.impressionPromptLimit` (default 10) in storage order. Repeated traits move to newest slot (`impressionMaxTraits` default 10, max 20); `impressionMaxMembers` (default 50) prunes longest unseen. Speaking advances `interactionCount`/`lastSeenAt` (not on replay); writes do not (`__proto__`-safe).
- **Wake delta payload:** current token authority, reason/instruction, unread count, wake config/timers (omits `sleepRangeMs`, `inheritsKeywords`, `customKeywordsFor`; wake-config writes ignore them), reply quiet/pre-sleep status, installed revision ref, fresh redacted console snapshot (exact IDs/cursors/partial metadata), tool-only reminder. Refusal provenance uses delivered packet only.

## Cache-aware session rotation

- **`session_rotation.rs` & hub:** rotates sleeping DSH conversations when last call's total input (cache hit+miss+write, incl. closing reminders) >= per-model `dsh.rotation.caps` (fallback `default`, else 500000), last completed turn confirmed wake config, and no open/paused turn, queued ingress/wake/retry, compaction or pinned CLI blocks maintenance (owner private uses same policy).
- **Due time:** `dsh.cacheTtlMinutes` (defaults: `local-relay` 5, `opencode-go` 120; unknown providers need explicit positive finite TTL). Idle starts at `max(lastIncoming, completedTurnEnd)`; due at `max(0, TTL - 1 min)`. Below-cap survives cache expiry. Incoming/policy/wake cancels/delays while slot stays owned until digest outcome/stop is known.
- **Digest & recovery:** runs Digest (`docs/agents/lifecycle-tasks.md`) only past `digestedThrough`; memories + cursor commit atomically (never submitted to DSH; restart reruns uncommitted; failed digest alerts once and still permits rotation; reset error marks attempt done to stop loops). Persists `rotate` phase separately from digest success; restart rechecks guards and resumes retirement without re-digesting. Failures record fixed step (`context-read`, `evidence`, `model`, `digest-result`, `memory-write`, `checkpoint`, `digest`) + kind in `sessions.json.rotationFailures` (`GET /api/sessions`). Partial-reset recovery keeps maintenance/ingress held until replacement token persists; 12 state-read failures emit diagnostic + daily-limited notice (`data/rotation-alerts.json` under sessions lock, process fallback) and retry every 60s.
- **Archive:** rechecks guards under social gate -> revokes mapping/token (preserving conv data/recent msgs) -> stops & archives DSH (no deletion) -> logs 1 activity line (conv, input tokens, digest yes/no). DSH mid-turn compaction safety net stays preset-owned (0.6 of 1M, retain 32000). See ADR 0007.

## Harness idle rotation

- **`harness/rotation.rs`:** checked at harness turn admission before first request (`due_at(cfg, last, idle_from)` using `cache_life_minutes` and `cap`). Idle starts at last turn end; due sessions rotate in place with summary + newest whole rounds within 4000 estimated tokens (`docs/agents/harness.md` "History budget and checkpoints"). Failed summary leaves unrotated.
- **Evidence (`harness::store::last_call`):** newest answered model attempt in current generation (by append-only seq) with recorded route id (legacy falls back to `harness.model` route id) and positive `captured_usage.prompt_tokens`. Unanswered or zero/missing input never triggers rotation.
- **Settings (isolated from DSH):** `harness.cacheTtlMinutes` per route id (positive finite; no built-in default, `dsh.cacheTtlMinutes` never applies -> unconfigured routes never idle-rotate); `harness.rotation.caps` per route id -> `default` -> 500000 (`dsh.rotation.caps` never applies). Due at `idle_from + ceil(max(0, cache_life - 1 min) ms)` (saturated `i64`).

## Bridge-enforced reply timing

- **In-turn suppression & urgent coverage:** running turns (incl. generation-matched recovery) suppress non-urgent wakes pre-trigger (`in-turn`). Urgent @/owner wakes use base queue/retries/quotas; same-generation completion drops queued urgent follow-up (`covered`) only when its exact seq appeared in that turn's successful tool response (wait, unread/history, mark-read ack) after all required writes succeed (revision sends require persisted revision budget, outbox supersession and settlement). Prompt snapshots, buffers, cursors, failed/cancelled/timed-out turns, restart and CLI grant no coverage.
- **Quiet & question exchange (`question.rs`):** idle quote replies retain `atMention` when enabled; in-turn quote without `@` is suppressed. `triggers.question` admits questions quoting her or from an active exchange participant within `socialV2.wake.questionExchangeWindowMs` (default 90000 ms) of her last message (evidence: member quote to her or confirmed bot reply/@ to member; excludes questions `@`ing others; nagging requires same exchange even when quoting her; AI words/aliases establish no direction; failed/uncertain sends establish no exchange).
- **Wake debounce:** `socialV2.reply.wakeQuietMs` (default 3000, 1-30000 ms, live next wake); every substantive arrival resets armed message wake up to 30000 ms from first unseen arrival; bot @mentions and owner trigger immediately.
- **Per-bubble quiet & typing holds:** enforced pre-bubble and pre-outbox on `qq_send_message`, burst, reply, direct console sends and text outbox resumes:
  - Quiet window: independent log-normal (log sigma 0.5) clamped to `[quietMinMs, quietMaxMs]` with `quietMedianMs` (defaults 4000/10000/25000 ms; urgent owner/@ uses `urgentQuiet*` 1000/3000/6000 ms; overrides 1-120000 ms; console refuses `!(min <= median <= max)`, load falls back per-field or full triple on ordering violation), measured from `lastReplyIncomingAt` (falls back to `lastIncomingAt`/substantive recent msg, else 0).
  - Typing budget: registered UTF-16 first-gap base + per-char + burst floor/gap cap generalized to all bubbles * uniform `[0.75, 1.25]` (test-seedable RNG, OS entropy in prod), credited by observed DSH thinking (since `started` or last delivered bubble; missing start -> 0 credit) or first harness request start. `firstGapEnabled=false` disables typing delay only; `fillTypingBudget=false` keeps full budget. Quiet and typing overlap (max, not sum).
  - Private held sends issue best-effort `set_input_status(user_id, 1)` (250 ms budget) and clear `0` on release/revision/revocation/failure; groups skip status.
- **`inbound_text::quiet_neutral`:** classifies pre-truncation inbound; neutral only if every segment is image/face/mface/bface/sface, quote wrapper around qualifying media/emoji, or whitespace/emoji-only text (incl. CQ codes and `[图片]`/`[表情…]` placeholders; digits/words/punct/quote-only/voice/video/file/unknown are substantive). Neutral arrivals stay in recent/unread and trigger held-send revision, but never extend `lastReplyIncomingAt` or quiet timers (`lastIncomingAt` and #94 wait batching still track them).
- **Held revisions & supersession:** arrival during hold cancels unsent bubbles pre-outbox and returns HTTP 200 `{held: true, reviseRequired: true, sent, newMessages, receipt/cursor, "对方又说了…"}` (retained delivered bubbles never resent; partial packet uses `nextAfterSeq`, `readThroughSeq` reflects offered only; spends no wait slot or sleep observation). Max 3 revisions/turn-token epoch (`replyRevisionBudget`); beyond 3, fixed deadline ignores later arrivals. Pre-return writes 1 durable outbox `superseded` record covering remaining indices (failure -> 503; legacy per-item `superseded` readable), releasing quota and excluding from resume/recovery; authority cancellation remains `cancelled`.
- **Prompt & schema contract:** `hf119_reply_prompt_leaves_timing_to_the_bridge` verifies `her/tests/fixtures/qq-chat-v2-reply-timing.yml` (`persona.config.prefix`). `socialV2.send.mergeMultiIntoOne` (default true) joins parts with `\n` in 1 bubble; when false, `burstEnabled` allows split parts <= `burstMaxMessages` with inter-bubble gaps; every bubble/merged text must fit `maxMessageChars` UTF-16. `[表情N]` allowlist/normalization follows `docs/agents/media-transport.md`.
- **Send caps & repeat guards:** executed in shared console batch executor pre-quota/intent/hold. Zero minute/hour quota disables that quota (defaults 8/60, cap 500). `socialV2.send.repeatLimit` (default 2) and `maxPerTurn` (default 20; positive safe integers) refuse violating batches with HTTP 429 (no hold/attempt/revision), logging only key/turn scope/reason/count and emitting <=1 durable content-free owner notice per turn. `repeatWindowSends` (default 0 = previous turn/scope; >0 = last N conversation outbox sends; current-turn repeats always count) compares canonical text across confirmed/uncertain typed bot sends (definite failures/cancelled/superseded excluded); limits follow actual turn across token rotations (resumes spend submitting turn cap; restart/timeout never erase outbox).
- **Reserved2 CLI relay:** requires companion console; 1s connect+write budget, 300s total budget (permits holds); transport failure logs `CLI reply send failed: companion console unavailable` and fails turn without OneBot fallback. Passes answer prose directly; retains turn token; audits final canonical face-normalized text via outbound sentinel + known-secret check. Each held revision carries cumulative bounded new-message packets (<=3 revisions; discarded drafts spend no send cap); HTTP 429 ends CLI turn without DSH fallback/alert; empty initial draft may fall back to DSH, empty revised/`[SILENT]` completes without send; blank process output = empty-response failure (`docs/agents/dependency-recovery.md`).
